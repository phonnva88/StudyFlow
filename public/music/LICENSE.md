# StudyFlow original ambient audio

Quiet Orbit and Soft Morning were created specifically for StudyFlow by the
procedural synthesis source in `scripts/render-study-music.mjs`. They contain
original chord arrangements and synthesized sine tones, with no sampled or
third-party recordings. Each file is 45 seconds, mono PCM WAV at 22050 Hz.

These compositions, generated recordings, and the synthesis script are dedicated
to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Public web playback, copying, sharing, modification, and redistribution are
permitted without attribution. No external music service is involved.
